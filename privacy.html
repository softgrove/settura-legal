Settura

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 17, 2026 · Last updated: September 15, 2026

Local content, limited anonymous usage data. Everything you enter stays on your device and is never sent to us. The app sends a small set of anonymous usage events to our own Cloudflare-hosted server so we can improve the app. There is no advertising, no third-party tracking, and no third-party analytics SDK.

Who we are

Settura (“the app”, “we”) is provided by Softgrove, an independent developer based in Japan. Contact: softgrove@duck.com.

What data the app handles

You may record meals, foods, symptoms, stool entries, daily factors (stress, sleep), and notes (“your entries”). Your entries are stored only locally on your device using Apple’s on-device database, protected with iOS file protection. We never receive this data.

We do not collect your entries, photos, notes, health or other user content, name, email address, account credentials (the app has no account), location, contacts, or advertising identifiers through the app. The limited usage events described below are the only data sent to us.

Anonymous usage analytics

Settura sends a small set of funnel events. An event name describes an internal action such as using a core feature, viewing a result or Pro offer, starting or completing a purchase, restoring a purchase, or interacting with a reminder. Not every version sends every event.

Each request contains the app identifier (a), app version (v), a random installation identifier (d), event name (e), timestamp (t), and a small properties object (p). Current properties are limited to fixed internal screen or flow names, an App Store product identifier, subscription plan, and trial-eligibility flag where relevant. They do not contain your entries, health information, photos, notes, or other free-form content.

The installation identifier is a random UUID generated on your device. It is not your Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), Apple vendor identifier (IDFV), name, email address, or account ID. We use these events only to understand aggregate feature use and funnel completion and to improve Settura; never for advertising or cross-app or cross-website tracking.

Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the endpoint as our infrastructure processor. Our Worker does not store source IP addresses, User-Agent headers, raw event bodies, raw timestamps, or raw installation identifiers. It stores daily aggregate counts and, for this app, a per-day HMAC-SHA-256 hash of the random identifier. The hash is salted with the day and app identifier, so it changes each day. Cloudflare may process network metadata to deliver and secure its service under its data-processing terms.

Health information

Your entries may relate to your health. Because this information never leaves your device through us — we do not access, transmit, or share it — we are not in a position to use it for any purpose. You remain in control: you can export or delete all entries at any time in Settings.

For Washington State residents (My Health My Data Act): we do not collect, process, share, or sell “consumer health data.” Your entries are stored solely on your device under your control.

Export and deletion

  • Export: You can export your entries (as a file) from Settings. Once you export and share a file, that copy is under your control and outside the app.
  • Delete: You can permanently delete all entries at any time from Settings → Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes its on-device data.

Subscriptions and payments

Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple’s handling of your purchase is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data is sent to us or any third party.

Children

Settura is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region) and collects no information from anyone.

Third parties

Settura has no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We use Cloudflare, Inc. only to host the anonymous event endpoint and aggregated database described above. We do not sell these events, provide them to third parties for their own purposes, use them for advertising, or use them to track you across apps or websites.
